The loop

Show up, get rated, get rewarded

The same three steps every week. Nothing to fill in by hand: the coach taps attendance on court and the rest follows.

01

Coach checks you in

Attendance is tapped on court. Points land in your account before you leave, plus a bonus for being on time.

02

Your card updates

Every six weeks the coach scores bandeja, víbora, wall exits and positioning. You see the change, not just the number.

03

Spend at the kantine

Batido, overgrip, a tube of balls or club kit. Show the redemption code at the bar, staff tap confirm.
