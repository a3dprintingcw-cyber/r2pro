Common questions

Before you ask

Do players pay anything?

No. Player accounts are free and included in the club licence. You keep charging for training the way you already do.

Can we keep our own points rules?

Yes. Every value is editable: what a session is worth, bonuses, expiry, and the whole kantine menu.

What if a player trains at two clubs?

One Google login, two academies. The player switches between them and each club sees only its own data.

How long does setup take?

An afternoon. Import your player list, set the squads and the points rules, and your coaches can check in that same week.
