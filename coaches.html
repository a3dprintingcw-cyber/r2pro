What coaches do in the app

The coach side

Attendance on court

Open the squad, tap the players who showed up. Points and streaks update immediately.

Assessments

Score each skill out of 10 every six weeks and leave a note the player reads in their file.

Upload drills

Film a drill on the phone, set a level and the kit needed, publish it to the home training tab.
