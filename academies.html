What an academy controls

Set up once, runs itself

Squads & schedule

Groups by level, coaches assigned, session times per court. Players see only their own squad.

Points rules

Decide what a session, a camp day or an on-time arrival is worth, and whether points expire.

Kantine menu

Your own items and point prices. Staff confirm a redemption code at the bar in two taps.

Assessment sheet

The skills your coaches score, in your language. Padel terms come ready, add your own.

Coach accounts

Coaches check attendance on court, score assessments and upload home drills from their phone.

Branding

Logo, colours and academy name. Players see your club, not ours.
